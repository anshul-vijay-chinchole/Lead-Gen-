## Q1. Recruitment CRM/ATS built-in BD features: do they offer job-posting / hiring-signal lead feeds? Pricing.

### Takeaway
Mainstream agency CRMs/ATSs (Bullhorn, Loxo, Recruiterflow, Recruit CRM, Vincere, JobAdder, Firefish, Recruitly) give agencies a sales pipeline, sequencing and automation for BD. None of them surfaced a native "companies hiring in your niche" job-posting lead feed. That data comes from marketplace add-ons (for example Jobdigger or Agency Leads into Bullhorn) or from signals about existing contacts (Recruiterflow job-change alerts). The one exception found is **Signals (recruitwithsignals.com)**, a new AI-native recruitment CRM that sells "BD Signals" (hiring intent before jobs are advertised) inside the CRM at $139–$199/user/month.

### Inferences
- A small agency already paying $85–$199/user/month for a CRM gets pipeline tooling but usually **not a curated niche list of who is hiring this week**. That gap is what add-ons (Jobdigger, Agency Leads, Vacancysoft) and the proposed weekly report fill.
- AI-native CRMs (Signals) and agentic features (Recruiterflow AIRA, Bullhorn "Max") look set to bundle hiring signals into the CRM. Over 12–24 months this could commoditise basic "who's hiring" data for agencies on those platforms.
- Integration matters. Agency Leads already syncs into Bullhorn, so a CSV/email-only weekly report is weaker for Bullhorn shops unless it offers CRM-importable files.

## Q2. Recruiter BD tools, hiring-signal data products and "companies hiring now" lead sellers: pricing and target customers

### Takeaway
Many products already sell exactly "companies hiring now + decision-maker contacts" to recruiters. In the **US**, Agency Leads charges $295/month and adds 1,500+ leads a week. In the **UK**, Vacancysoft (since 2006) charges from £250/month, and Jobdigger sits in the Bullhorn Marketplace. In the **EU**, Leads for Recruiters sells pay-per-lead with a €350 pilot. On top of these there is a 2025–26 wave of AI "signal" startups (Recruit Signals, Hirelytiq, Signals, dreach, Oppora), cheap weekly funded-company lists (Fundraise Insider at $149–$299 one-off with lifetime weekly lists, VCBacked from $79/month), and DIY job-posting data APIs (TheirStack from $49–$98/month, JobsPikr $79–$240/month, Apify scrapers). The category is crowded. The remaining room is in niche depth, curation and price, not in the raw data.

### Cited Findings
**Recruiter-specific "hiring company" lead subscriptions**
- **Agency Leads (US staffing):** an "Agency Lead" is an open job at a company that uses staffing firms. The database has 225k–229k+ "verified hiring companies" and adds 1,500+ new leads weekly. Leads are "manually verified by their team" and include job details, company info and contact info (vendor claims) — [Agency Leads](https://agency-leads.com/); [Agency Leads solutions](https://agency-leads.com/en/solutions/agency-leads/)
- Agency Leads pricing: $95 for the first month, then $295/month. 6-month prepay saves 5% off the quarterly rate and annual prepay saves 20% — [Agency Leads pricing](https://agency-leads.com/en/pricing/)
- Agency Leads runs a large SEO content operation ("7 best lead generation tools for staffing agencies 2026", "Agency Leads vs ZoomInfo") and a Bullhorn integration — [Agency Leads tools list](https://agency-leads.com/recruitment-tools/best-lead-generation-tools-staffing-agencies-2026/); [Agency Leads vs ZoomInfo](https://agency-leads.com/recruitment-tools/agency-leads-vs-zoominfo-for-recruiters/)
- **Leads for Recruiters (EU, € pricing):** pay-per-lead with no contract, starting with a €350 pilot and automatic volume discounts; orders can be as small as 1 lead. Provides verified hiring-manager contacts with direct mobile numbers, "verified within 24 hours of detection", from live job-posting signals. Claims a 5.6% conversion rate and GDPR compliance (vendor claims). Guarantee: a wrong mobile number is replaced free, and a job that turns out to be posted by a recruitment agency is replaced — [Leads for Recruiters pricing](https://leadsforrecruiters.com/pricing/); [Leads for Recruiters](https://leadsforrecruiters.com/)
- **Vacancysoft (UK, est. 2006):** "UK's leading provider of labour market data" (vendor claim). "Vacancy Tracker" is a tailored daily feed of vacancy leads, with alerts on vacancies appearing on client websites. Priced by data package with "unlimited users for a singular price"; a single-user licence starts at £250/month — [Vacancysoft](https://vacancysoft.com/); [Vacancy Tracker](https://vacancysoft.com/vacancy-tracker/); [Vacancysoft data publishers](https://vacancysoft.com/28-2/)
- **Jobdigger:** vacancy lead and market-trend data for recruitment agencies, on Bullhorn Marketplace, with a "95% reliability" claim. Pricing not found — [Bullhorn Marketplace: Jobdigger](https://www.bullhorn.com/marketplace/jobdigger/)

**AI "hiring signal" startups aimed at agencies (2025–26 wave)**

Delivers leads with verified decision-maker contacts and a suggested outreach angle. Claims tech/IT agencies closed retained or exclusive assignments from signal accounts in 19 days on average versus 31 days for cold calling (vendor claim, methodology unknown). A pricing page exists but figures were not captured — [Recruit Signals](https://recruitsignals.com/); [Recruit Signals pricing](https://recruitsignals.com/pricing); [RocketReach profile](https://rocketreach.co/recruit-signals-profile_b6990d17c8acb38e)

It offers a free "intelligence snapshot". Promoted by a syndicated press release dated 2026-01-05 (AB Newswire). Pricing not found — [Hirelytiq](https://hirelytiq.com/); [Hirelytiq snapshot](https://hirelytiq.com/intelligence-snapshot); [FinancialContent/AB Newswire release](https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/abnewswire-2026-1-5-how-early-hiring-signals-are-changing-recruitment-business-development)

A testimonial says it replaced an Instantly + Apollo stack costing about $200/month (vendor claim) — [Oppora pricing](https://oppora.ai/pricing/); [Oppora: best tool for recruitment agency BD](https://oppora.ai/blog/best-tool-for-recruitment-agency-business-development/)

Lusha and Kaspr are recommended for small agencies (Chrome extension on LinkedIn), and ZoomInfo/Cognism for enterprise and MSP deals (Agency Leads' own comparison content) — [Agency Leads: 2026 BD stack](https://agency-leads.com/recruiter-tips/recruiter-tools-for-lead-generation/); [Agency Leads tools list](https://agency-leads.com/recruitment-tools/best-lead-generation-tools-staffing-agencies-2026/)
- **Getlead:** generic B2B scraper plus cold email sold as a lifetime deal ($9.90 / $49.90 / $99.90 one-time; Starter includes 1,000 leads/month). Explicitly pitched to recruiters for filtering HR/TA titles — [Getlead pricing](https://getle.ad/pricing); [Getlead: tools for recruiters](https://getle.ad/blog/best-lead-generation-tools-for-recruiters)

**Hunted (hunted.com)**
- Search summaries describe Hunted as a recruitment-industry platform with company profiles, live job slots, a supplier directory and recruiter career tools, sold by subscription. It is **not** clearly a BD lead feed, and the summary is ambiguous — [Hunted](https://www.hunted.com/); [Hunted pricing](https://www.hunted.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- The price ladder for recruiter-specific "who's hiring" data runs from lifetime deals ($149–$299 Fundraise Insider, $9.90–$99.90 Getlead) through low-cost subscriptions ($34–$79 Oppora, $79 VCBacked, $49–$98 TheirStack DIY) to mid-market feeds (£250/month Vacancysoft, $295/month Agency Leads). Pay-per-lead (Leads for Recruiters) and CRM-bundled signals ($139–$199/user Signals) sit alongside. A weekly niche report priced around $49–$149/month would sit in a crowded low-to-mid band. It needs a clear non-price reason to exist.
- Hygiene features the brief mentions (excluding agency-posted jobs, verification, replacement guarantees, freshness) are **already marketed** by Agency Leads (manual verification, companies known to use agencies) and Leads for Recruiters (24-hour verification, agency-post filter, free replacement). They are table stakes, not differentiators.
- Most funded-company products (Fundraise Insider, VCBacked, Hirelytiq, Recruit Signals) sell *predictive* signals. Agency Leads, Vacancysoft, Jobdigger and Leads for Recruiters sell *live vacancies*. A report combining live postings in one niche with hiring-manager identification and "why this matters" context could sit between the two.

### Gaps
- Could not get prices for Recruit Signals, Hirelytiq, dreach, Jobdigger or Launch Leads (pages blocked, search budget exhausted).
- Tools named in the brief but not researched: JobsPikr's higher tiers, Hireflow, Dripify, Expandi, Overloop pricing, Pitchme, Veloxy, Sonar, PredictLeads, Coresignal. (Dripify and Expandi are generic LinkedIn automation, not recruiter-specific. Their pricing was not retrieved.)
- No independent (non-vendor) reviews of Agency Leads, Vacancysoft, Leads for Recruiters or Recruit Signals were retrieved. G2/Capterra/Trustpilot pages were blocked.
- The Agency Leads "225k" and "229k" figures differ slightly across its own pages, probably from updates over time.

## Q3. Done-for-you lead generation / BD agencies for recruitment firms: offers, pricing models, claimed results

### Takeaway
Many outsourced SDR and appointment-setting agencies have recruitment/staffing verticals (Belkins, Launch Leads, CallingAgency, SalesBread, Zaphyre, LevelUp Leads) alongside recruiter-specific GTM builders (Recruitcha GTM, RecruiterGTM). Pricing is mostly by retainer, roughly **$2,000–$15,000/month** (Belkins), which is far above what a 1–5 person niche agency spends. That leaves room below it for a "done-for-you research, you do the outreach" product.

### Cited Findings
- **Recruitcha GTM (recruitcha.ai):** "go-to-market infrastructure for staffing agencies", building signal-based systems for client acquisition and candidate sourcing. Uses "junior recruiters, signal-qualified lists, and fast human follow-up" on LinkedIn. Blog content covers building an automated outbound system "for under $100/month" and domain/deliverability mistakes agencies make. Pricing not found. *Recruitcha.com is a separate B2B SaaS recruitment agency; do not conflate the two* — [Recruitcha GTM](https://www.recruitcha.ai/); [Recruitcha GTM blog](https://www.recruitcha.ai/blog); [Recruitcha (recruitment agency)](https://www.recruitcha.com/)
- G2 lists a "Recruitcha" pricing page, but no figures were surfaced — [G2 Recruitcha pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/recruitcha/pricing)
- **RecruiterGTM:** sells a "5-Pillar GTM Framework for Recruitment Agencies" — [RecruiterGTM](https://www.recruitergtm.com/)
- **Launch Leads:** runs appointment setting for recruitment and staffing firms. It qualifies prospects on open headcount, hiring timeline and "vendor dissatisfaction", with outreach by phone, email and LinkedIn to VP HR, CHROs, CEOs and TA Directors. First qualified appointments land "in Week 3-4" (vendor claim). Positioned as "open-req HR leaders" / "open-req hiring managers". Pricing not published in retrieved snippets — [Launch Leads recruitment agencies](https://www.launchleads.com/industries/recruitment-agencies/); [Launch Leads staffing](https://www.launchleads.com/industries/staffing/)

"Starts at $8,000/month for 100 guaranteed appointments/year and 3 outreach channels." One review computes about $1,528 per appointment at $10,700/month over 3 months (competitor/third-party reviews) — [Belkins recruitment & staffing](https://belkins.io/industries/recruitment-staffing); [SalesBread: Belkins review](https://salesbread.com/belkins-io/) (competitor); [Salesforge: Belkins review](https://www.salesforge.ai/blog/belkins-review-2026-b2b-lead-generation-appointment-setting-agency); [OutboundSalesPro](https://outboundsalespro.com/belkins-review/)

- **CallingAgency:** "qualified leads and appointments with hiring decision-makers for staffing". Pricing not captured — [CallingAgency recruitment leads](https://callingagency.com/lead-generation-services/recruitment-and-staffing-agency-leads/)
- **Zaphyre (zaphyrpro.com):** US staffing lead gen claiming "20+ years" — [Zaphyre](https://zaphyrpro.com/recruitment-and-staffing); **LevelUp Leads** HR and staffing vertical — [LevelUp Leads](https://levelupleads.io/industries-served/hr-staffing/); **LeadHaste** publishes a "best lead gen agency for staffing 2026" list — [LeadHaste](https://leadhaste.com/blog/best-lead-generation-agency-for-staffing-2026)
- Price benchmarks quoted by vendors: managed outbound programmes "typically range from roughly $2,500 to $19,000 per month", with qualified leads "between $84 and $400 each" (Fundraise Insider marketing comparison) — [Fundraise Insider pricing](https://fundraiseinsider.com/pricing/)
- A search summary cited an average staffing/recruiting cost per lead of $497 (range $476–$518) and a "healthy BD budget" of 10–15% of agency revenue. The specific source article among the results could not be confirmed, so treat these as unverified — [Woodpecker: pay-per-lead companies](https://woodpecker.co/blog/pay-per-lead-generation-companies/); [Leonar: staffing lead gen](https://www.leonar.app/blog/lead-generation-staffing-agencies/); [Agency Leads: lead gen for agencies](https://agency-leads.com/recruiter-tips/lead-generation-for-agencies/)

### Inferences
- Done-for-you appointment setting for recruiters is well served at the **$2k+/month** level. Few players occupy the **sub-$200/month "done-for-you research"** tier (curated list plus context, where the agency sends the outreach). This is the tier a weekly niche report would sit in.
- Pay-per-meeting and retainer agencies need a large contract value to pay off. A solo specialised recruiter billing a few placements a year is poorly matched to a $5k/month retainer, which is the opening for a cheap product.

### Gaps
- No pay-per-meeting price points for recruitment-specific agencies were confirmed (e.g., $/meeting for Launch Leads or CallingAgency).
- Recruitcha GTM's pricing and claimed results were not retrieved.
- No UK-specific done-for-you recruitment BD agencies were identified. The search budget ran out before UK-focused queries.

## Q4. Freelancers on Upwork/Fiverr selling "hiring companies lead lists" / "job posting leads for recruiters": prices and reviews

### Takeaway
Fiverr has a dedicated "recruitment leads" gig category. Search summaries mention gigs building lists of companies "actively hiring" **starting from $25**. ## Q5. Saturation assessment: number of players, differentiation, demand evidence, and gaps a cheap curated weekly niche report could fill

### Takeaway
The niche is **crowded and getting more so**. This research alone found about 9 CRMs with BD pipelines (one with native hiring signals), about 10 recruiter-specific hiring-lead or signal products, about 4 generic job-data APIs and scrapers, 7+ done-for-you agencies, and a Fiverr/Upwork freelancer tier, plus a heavy 2026 SEO content push on "signal-based BD for recruitment agencies". The raw product ("companies hiring + contacts") is commoditised. Remaining gaps are **niche depth, human curation with context, tight dedup/exclusivity, honest verification, and a price between $25 one-off lists and $250–$295/month feeds**. Direct demand evidence from practitioner communities (Reddit) could not be collected.

### Cited Findings
- Count of recruiter-specific "hiring lead" products found: Agency Leads, Leads for Recruiters, Vacancysoft, Jobdigger, Recruit Signals, Hirelytiq, Signals (CRM), dreach, Oppora, Fundraise Insider, VCBacked (sources in Q1–Q2), e.g. [Agency Leads](https://agency-leads.com/), [Vacancysoft](https://vacancysoft.com/), [Recruit Signals](https://recruitsignals.com/), [Hirelytiq](https://hirelytiq.com/), [Leads for Recruiters](https://leadsforrecruiters.com/)
- Signals of a content-marketing arms race: a syndicated press release (2026-01-05) titled "How Early Hiring Signals Are Changing Recruitment Business Development" plus many 2026 vendor guides on BD signals for agencies — [FinancialContent/AB Newswire](https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/abnewswire-2026-1-5-how-early-hiring-signals-are-changing-recruitment-business-development); [Signals 2026 guide](https://recruitwithsignals.com/insights/bd-signals-recruitment-agencies/); [Recruit Signals: signal BD vs cold outreach](https://recruitsignals.com/intelligence/articles/recruitment-agency-sales-signal-bd-vs-cold-outreach); [Recruit Signals: hiring signals in a quiet market](https://recruitsignals.com/intelligence/articles/hiring-signals-quiet-market-bd); [Oppora: 11 best staffing lead gen tools](https://oppora.ai/blog/staffing-agency-lead-generation-tools/)
- Vendors frame the market as "stabilising" or "quiet", with BD harder, and publish playbooks accordingly (vendor framing) — [Recruit Signals: BD playbook for a stabilising market](https://recruitsignals.com/intelligence/articles/recruitment-agency-bd-playbook-stabilising-market)
- For niche agencies, vendor content calls "first-time roles in their niche" the "highest-conversion BD opportunity", and clusters of related postings (e.g., three hires in one department in 30 days) signal budget and urgency (vendor claims) — [Recruit Signals: contact hiring managers 30 days before job ads](https://recruitsignals.com/intelligence/articles/contact-hiring-managers-30-days-before-job-ads-appear-win); [Execue: recruitment lead signals playbook](https://execue.io/blog/recruitment-lead-signals-contact-company)
- Anecdotal demand evidence: a sales-coaching newsletter case study describes an experienced biotech/pharma recruiter "struggling to gain traction" in BD, with unclear targeting and only calling top executives — [The Honest Salesperson #094](https://thehonestsalesperson.substack.com/p/ths-094-how-one-recruiter-turned)
- Content aimed at BD pain is abundant (cold-call scripts for recruiters from CRM vendors) — [Recruit CRM: cold calling scripts](https://recruitcrm.io/blogs/recruitment-cold-calling-scripts-for-business-development/); [Recruit CRM: how to generate recruitment leads](https://recruitcrm.io/blogs/recruitment-leads/)
- Incumbents already advertise hygiene features: excluding agency-posted jobs and free replacement ([Leads for Recruiters](https://leadsforrecruiters.com/pricing/)); manual verification and companies known to use agencies ([Agency Leads](https://agency-leads.com/)); daily tailored vacancy feeds ([Vacancysoft Vacancy Tracker](https://vacancysoft.com/vacancy-tracker/))
- Weekly-delivery analogue at a very low one-off price: Fundraise Insider's $149–$299 lifetime weekly lists — [Fundraise Insider pricing](https://fundraiseinsider.com/pricing/)

### Inferences
- **Saturation level: high for generic "who's hiring" data, moderate for niche-curated human-reviewed reports.** Nearly every product found is either broad (all industries or all staffing, e.g. Agency Leads, TheirStack, Fundraise Insider) or predictive/AI (Recruit Signals, Hirelytiq, Signals). Few surfaced as a *human-curated, single-niche, weekly* digest for 1–10 person specialist agencies.
- **Possible gaps for a cheap curated weekly niche report:**
  1. *Niche depth.* One report per micro-niche (e.g., "UK med-device QA/RA roles" or "US Series A–C fintech engineering"), with filters a generalist feed doesn't apply: first-time role in function, repeat or re-posted roles (hard to fill), clusters.
  2. *Context and angle.* A "why now" line and likely hiring manager per company, which is what Recruit Signals sells via AI.
  3. *Dedup and exclusivity.* Week-over-week dedup, plus capping subscribers per niche and region so leads aren't shared with 50 competitors. This counters the shared-list problem of mass databases. (Inference; no source found discussing lead-sharing saturation.)
  4. *Honest verification.* Clearly labelling email status (verified / catch-all / guessed) instead of blanket "verified" claims. Competitors advertise verification, but no independent audit was found.
  5. *Price.* Roughly $49–$149/month sits below Vacancysoft (£250) and Agency Leads ($295) and above Fiverr ($25 one-off). But it competes head-on with Fundraise Insider's lifetime deal, VCBacked ($79/month), Oppora ($34–$79) and DIY TheirStack ($49–$98/month).
- **Main risks:**
  - (a) AI-native CRMs bundling signals.
  - (b) Low-price anchors from lifetime deals.
  - (c) Mid-market incumbents like Agency Leads offering a $95 first month.
  - (d) The report being easy to replicate with TheirStack plus Apollo by a tech-savvy agency. The buyers least able to replicate it (non-technical small specialists) are the natural target.
- **UK vs US:** the UK has an entrenched vacancy-data incumbent (Vacancysoft, since 2006, £250/month+) and Jobdigger. In the US the recruiter-specific lead feed is led by Agency Leads ($295/month). Both leave the sub-£/$150 niche-curated tier relatively open, based on what was found.

### Gaps
- **No Reddit or community demand evidence was collected.** Reddit could not be fetched, and site-restricted searches returned no Reddit threads. Practitioner quotes on BD pain, willingness to pay, or complaints about Agency Leads/ZoomInfo/Apollo data quality remain unverified.
- No customer counts, churn or revenue for any recruiter-specific lead product, so real demand volume can't be gauged.
- No independent review data (G2/Capterra/Trustpilot scores and review text) for the recruiter lead products.
- No podcast or industry-analyst (e.g., Staffing Industry Analysts, Recruitment International) commentary on BD tooling saturation was retrieved.
